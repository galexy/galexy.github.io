The other day, I write a short snippet ranting about ai-(douters|haters). My chief concern is that shutting the door to AI is its own virtuous (wrong connotation) cycle that leads to a lack of imagination on how to leverage AI. 

But, today, the Glama maintainers [talk about a strategy to detect bot contributions](https://glama.ai/blog/2026-03-19-open-source-has-a-bot-problem). I really appreciate the attempt to actually come up with solutions to the current state of the world instead of just shutting the door. This is imaginative solution (albeit temporary) and not just throwing the baby out with the bath water. His attitude is the right, one - [asking how do I leverage this](https://glama.ai/blog/2026-03-19-open-source-has-a-bot-problem#:~:text=But%20the%20more%20interesting%20question%20is:%20now%20that%20I%20can%20identify%20the%20bots%2C%20can%20I%20make%20them%20do%20extra%20work%20that%20would%20make%20their%20contributions%20genuinely%20valuable?)?
