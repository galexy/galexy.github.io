This blog is now officially published. It's built on top of [Astro](https://astro.build/), but almost all the
code for driving this site was vibe coded in under a day. \~I spent the afternoon\~Claude Code ported
the CSS from the [terminal theme](https://astro.build/themes/details/astro-terminal/) to tailwind.
This blog has various types of content, modeled after [Simon Willison's amazing blog](https://simonwillison.net/),
including posts, snippets, quotes and webmarks.

I've always wanted to learn in public, but with all the other urgent and important things taking my
time, I never got to write a blog. (Actually, I had one for six months before I joined Microsoft).
I recently just left Thunk.ai and have a ton of time to learning cool new things. I hope to share
what I learn here. My goal is to post something at least 5 times a week (even if it's just short
meaningless snippets like this one.)
