Just read about [vouch](https://github.com/mitchellh/vouch) and realized
it's much related to something I've been thinking about in the past 
couple of days.

Like everyone else that is getting up to speed with coding agents, I've
been thinking about what software "creation" will look like going forward.
If we really get to the point where we can [create system without looking
at the code](https://factory.strongdm.ai), what will the interface for
a software creator look like? In Star Trek, everyone just talks to the
computer and Gen AI creates a simulation in the holodeck. 

Until then, what will our creation canvas look like? If we aren't looking at the
code and bridging the outcomes and objectives to the code becomes the role
of us humans, then will we be just writing specs or speclets (if your not
into waterfall)?

Will this actually revive open source? I feel like in the past few years
open source has caused a lot of burn out. Tons of issues being reported
and PRs that don't go reviewed for days, weeks, months. But, what if the
bug reports and the ideas are what other contribute and the maintainer 
acts like the benevolent product dictator and decides what issues and
features/epics get sent to the agent swarm?

What is the role of the PRs then? Do we really need system like 
[vouch](https://github.com/mitchellh/vouch)?
