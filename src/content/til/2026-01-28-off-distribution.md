I came across Anthropic's Claude Code prompting [cookbook for front-ends](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-cookbooks/blob/main/coding/prompting_for_frontend_aesthetics.ipynb).
What really stood out for me was telling Claude that it typically would generate "on distribution" / boring UIs.
In otherwords, what people would consider "AI slop" aesthetic. By pointing this out and asking it to
"Avoid this: make creative, distinctive frontends that surprise and delight.", is a pretty
neat trick.
